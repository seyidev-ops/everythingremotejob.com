LinkedIn DMs vs Cold Email: Which Gets More Replies in 2026

The data from 200+ outreach campaigns. LinkedIn DMs vs cold email — which channel wins in 2026 and when to use each one.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 5 June 2026 4 min read

The Data From 200+ Campaigns

Over two years tracking outreach results with remote job seekers, the numbers consistently point in one direction.

LinkedIn: The 2026 Leader

  • Average connection acceptance rate: 35–55%
  • Reply rate to follow-up DM after acceptance: 25–40%
  • Time to first reply: 2–4 days
  • Best for: startups, scale-ups, hiring managers active on LinkedIn

Cold Email: Still Relevant, Different Use Case

  • Average open rate with a strong subject line: 30–50%
  • Reply rate: 8–20%
  • Time to first reply: 3–7 days
  • Best for: senior roles, companies with public email formats, managers less active on LinkedIn

The Verdict

LinkedIn wins for most remote job seekers in 2026. The connection request creates a warm context that cold email cannot replicate — the person has chosen to connect before seeing your ask, which changes the psychology of the follow-up entirely.

The Winning Combination

LinkedIn first. If no acceptance within 5 days, email as backup using Hunter.io or Apollo.io to find the format. Never both simultaneously — it reads as desperate. Sequential, not parallel.
